Popup Markets

2024-current

After the success of the merchandise line on San Juan island, I started selling merchandise back home as well. Using my own artwork, I made and sold custom keychains and clothing at pop-ups starting in california. It started out with gigs at philz coffee and lululemon. Over time I saw that small souvenirs like keychains and handbags were selling the best at in perosn pop-ups, so that became the main focus for future events. I’ve consistently held pop-ups at philz coffee ever since, as well as back home at events with public schools, FAD market, Corepower, Redeemer brooklyn and other events across the country. Kids and families really enjoy making their own charms and customizing their own souvenirs. My favorite part about this venture is that I can make real impact with my own artwork. Seeing people enjoy my artwork makes me feel fulfilled with my creativity, like I am putting it to good use.
