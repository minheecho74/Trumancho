Analog Angry Birds

The first time I played a video game was when I was 4 years old. I vividly remember standing outside of my preschool playing “angry birds” on my mom’s phone while waiting for the bus. It was unlike anything I’ve experienced before in my three years of life. But when I couldn’t play on my mom’s phone, I was bored. All I wanted to do was play angry birds. The picture above was my solution to my boredom. Using spare legos and toy blocks, I made my own “angry birds” game that I could play without a phone. Ever since then, I’ve had the interest to create real video games, and respark that feeling from when I was three years old.
