How it started

Kindergarten 2016

My interest in drawing and storytelling started when I was little. I was at the park after school with to my friends, when we saw some older kids selling their own hand drawn comic books for a dollar a piece. The picture below is me reading the book for the first time after I bought one. This is when I realized that I could do things like this myself. Seeing other kids make and sell things that they made themselves inspired me to do the same.
