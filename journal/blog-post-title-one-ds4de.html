Kyoto Recap

During the summer of 2025, I was able to have the once in a lifetime opportunity to study abroad in Kyoto, Japan to learn video game design. This was not only an amazing way for me to spend my summer vacation exploring a new city and eating tons of good food, but also to grow my main passion and skills that I've been developing since I was little. During the trip, I was able to meet a lot of new people, learn many new things, as well as make so many lifetime memories. I remember how nervous I was on the days leading up to the trip, as this was the first time I would be living abroad without my family. However those feelings immediately fled on the first day of the program. Now looking back, I am so glad I decided to go. This trip is something I will never forget!
