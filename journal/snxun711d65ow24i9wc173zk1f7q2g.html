First Game Jam- Blood Moon

2023

This contest taught me the importance of time management and organization when working on a project. Unlike previous games I had made, this game had a deadline. This meant I had to plamn everything out ahead of time, something I did not efficiently do at first. By the end of the contest, my game felt very rushed and unpolished, however I learned from this experience for the future. I now understand the importance of both physical and mental organization, which I carried with me into the following game competition.

What this says about me is that I truly enjoy making games, and I strive to learn from past experiences.
